LinkedIn Recruiter Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

LinkedIn Recruiter Tracker is a personal, non-commercial application intended to help its developer organize their own professional conversations and job opportunities. This policy explains the data the application may access and how it is handled during its initial testing phase.

1. Information accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application may retrieve data made available by LinkedIn's Member Data Portability API, including:

  • LinkedIn messages sent and received by the account holder;
  • Conversation participants and related profile identifiers;
  • Message and conversation identifiers, timestamps, and related metadata.

2. How information is used

The information is used solely for personal testing and, if development continues, organizing recruiter conversations and tracking job opportunities. The application does not send automated LinkedIn messages or publish conversation contents.

3. Storage and security

During the initial proof-of-concept, API responses are processed locally on the developer's computer. The test is designed not to persist message contents by default. Access credentials are kept out of source control and must not be included in public logs.

If persistent storage or cloud infrastructure is added later, this policy will be updated to describe those practices before deployment.

4. Sharing and third parties

The application does not sell personal information. During the initial testing phase, LinkedIn message contents are not intentionally transmitted to third-party analytics or AI services. Any future use of external processing services will be disclosed here before it is enabled.

5. Retention and deletion

The initial test is designed not to retain message contents after execution. Any local files deliberately created for debugging or testing can be deleted by the developer. Access can be revoked through the relevant LinkedIn account settings.
